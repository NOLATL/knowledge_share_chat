# Volume 7 — Treasury & Cash Management Handbook

---

## 1. What is this function?
Treasury & Cash Management is the corporate finance function responsible for managing an enterprise’s cash liquidity, banking relationships, capital structure, and financial risk profiles. It encompasses daily cash positioning, short-term debt and investment administration, multi-bank ledger clearings, currency hedging (FX), and the architecture of the 13-Week Rolling Cash Forecast.

## 2. Why does it exist?
Treasury exists to guarantee corporate solvency and optimize the velocity of working capital. It protects the company's financial health and capital preservation by continuously addressing three macro questions:
* **Do we have the exact liquid funds required, in the right currency and right account, to meet today’s operating obligations?** (Solvency & Liquidity Optimization)
* **How can we maximize yield on idle corporate cash while preserving capital?** (Capital Preservation & Asset Allocation)
* **Are we mitigating corporate exposure to volatile macroeconomic markets?** (FX, Interest Rate, and Counterparty Risk Management)

## 3. Who performs it?
The function is executed by a specialized corporate treasury team working closely with commercial banks:
* **Treasury Analysts / Cash Managers:** Oversees daily bank balance polling, executes wires, manages cash concentrations, and handles bank portal security administration.
* **Corporate Treasurer:** Directs capital markets strategies, manages credit line allocations, establishes hedging policies, and maintains commercial banking partnerships.
* **Leadership:** The Treasurer and Vice President of Finance, reporting straight to the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## 4. How does it work?

### Core Treasury Methodologies
Treasury functions as the core control hub for corporate monetary mechanics, executing structural cash rules across the global banking footprint.

| Sub-Process | Focus | Structural Characteristics | Impact Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Positioning** | Aggregating intraday multi-bank balances and calculating net clearings. | High-frequency logging, relying on MT940/BAI2 standard bank telemetry logs. | Real-time Working Capital |
| **Liquidity Forecasting** | Assembling look-ahead cash runways using operational projections. | Traditionally manual, spreadsheet-heavy models compiled every week. | 13-Week Cash Runway |
| **FX & Risk Mitigation** | Executing spot trades, forwards, and derivative hedges to offset currency shifts. | Linked closely to volatile international macro markets. | Margin Erosion Protection |
| **Bank Structure Governance** | Managing multi-bank KYC, account mandates, and token system authorizations. | Rigid security structures prone to administrative friction. | Fraud & Access Control |

#### Net Daily Cash Position Formula
To manage cash velocity, Treasury must calculate available cash clearings at the start of every business morning:
$$\text{Net Cash Position} = \text{Available Bank Balances} - \text{Uncleared Disbursements} + \text{Inbound Cleared Receipts}$$

#### Days Cash on Hand (DCOH)
A critical runway safety metric compiled by Treasury to track how long an enterprise can sustain operations using current cash reserves without adding new revenue:
$$\text{Days Cash on Hand} = \frac{\text{Cash + Short Term Marketable Securities}}{(\text{Total Operating Expenses} - \text{Non-Cash Expenses}) \div 365}$$

### The Lifespan of a Day in Treasury: A Narrative Journey
The rhythm of a Treasury desk operates on strict daily bank cut-off times. Unlike accounting functions tied to month-end cycles, Treasury undergoes a high-stakes cash crunch every single morning.

#### Phase 1: The Morning Multi-Bank Balance Poll (07:00 – 09:00)
The morning kicks off with a race to establish the global cash baseline. A Treasury analyst logs into multiple disconnected corporate banking portals (e.g., Bank of America, HSBC, Citibank) utilizing hardware security tokens. 

They download the latest statement data files, typically formatted in rigid legacy structures like **BAI2** or **MT940** text strings. The data is pulled together into a master spreadsheet to calculate the net available balances across all international entities and subsidiaries.

#### Phase 2: Cash Concentration & Zero-Balance Account (ZBA) Sweeps (09:00 – 11:00)
Once balances are verified, Treasury initiates cash sweeps. Operating local subsidiary accounts often leaves cash fragmented across regional accounts. 

To maximize capital efficiency, Treasury triggers automated concentration sweeps, pulling idle balances out of local nodes upward into a master corporate concentration account. Conversely, they fund **Zero-Balance Accounts (ZBAs)** to ensure operational payment nodes can clear active checks or ACH runs generated by the P2P group later that afternoon.

#### Phase 3: The 13-Week Cash Forecast Update (11:00 – 13:00)
With the daily baseline secured, the focus shifts to compiling the **13-Week Rolling Cash Forecast**. This framework is the gold standard for liquidity monitoring. The analyst extracts accounts payable aging blocks from the P2P ledger, looks up invoice collection pipelines from the O2C engine, and overlays scheduled payroll chunks from Volume 6. 

They then adjust these lines with broad corporate assumptions (e.g., expected Capex spend, debt service deadlines). This forecast acts as the CFO's early warning radar to see if the company will cross under minimum liquidity thresholds over the next 90 days.

#### Phase 4: FX Vetting & Counterparty Risk Audits (13:00 – 15:00)
In the afternoon, Treasury addresses currency exposures. If a Latin American subsidiary collects revenues in Mexican Pesos (MXN) but the parent company incurs technology costs in US Dollars (USD), Treasury calculates the cross-currency exposure matrix. 

They interface with fx trading desks to purchase forward contracts or options, locking in exchange rates and immunizing corporate margins against international currency swings.

#### Phase 5: Payment Authorization & Portal Sign-Off (15:00 – 17:00)
Before bank clearing lines close for the evening, the Treasurer reviews all high-value corporate cash transfers. They audit the daily funding runs, check wire verification signatures, and log back into the banking networks to authorize multi-million dollar outlays. 

Once confirmed, the transaction records are packaged and passed downstream to the Record-to-Report (R2R) team to execute monthly cash journal ledger matching.

## 5. How does it interact with adjacent functions?

* **Procure-to-Pay (P2P):** Provides weekly AP aging projection files and maps automated check disbursement requirements to daily funding queues.
* **Order-to-Cash (O2C):** Provides daily lockbox, credit card clearings, and incoming wire notifications to confirm liquidity additions.
* **Payroll & Workforce Finance:** Flags large bi-weekly or monthly cash drawdown demands 48–72 hours prior to direct deposit releases.
* **FP&A:** Validates long-range strategic models against real-world 13-week operating cash runways, adjusting capital allocation metrics based on capital cost variations.

## 7. What data flows through it?

### Data Inputs
* **Bank Statement Telemetry:** End-of-day and intraday transactional logs delivered via SFTP or API channels (BAI2, MT940, camt.053 XML blocks).
* **Sub-Ledger Forecast Blocks:** Itemized accounts receivable forward pipelines (O2C) and committed accounts payable disbursement logs (P2P).
* **Macro Market Data Feeds:** Real-time spot currency exchange variables, SOFR interest indices, and sovereign bond yields.

### Data Outputs
* **Payment Execution Triggers:** Electronic payment instructions routing outwards to global networks (ISO 20022 XML formats, SWIFT MT103 lines).
* **13-Week Rolling Cash Matrix:** Consolidated liquidity forward report mapping net inflows and cash balances across 90-day segments.
* **FX Trade Settlements:** Trade matching receipts, hedging compliance papers, and forward derivative contracts documentation.

## 8. What KPIs matter?
* **Cash Forecasting Accuracy %:** The percentage delta between forecasted cash outcomes and actual bank balances at the end of a multi-week look-ahead segment.
* **Idle Cash Ratio %:** Total corporate capital sitting dormant in non-interest-bearing operating nodes relative to centralized interest-bearing vehicles.
* **Bank Fee Variance:** Uncontrolled costs or unexpected billing adjustments discovered across monthly bank service analysis sheets.
* **Time to Position Cash:** The total elapsed hours required every morning to pull global balances and calculate the net liquid baseline.

## 9. What are the pain points?
* **The "Multi-Bank Data Shrapnel" Slowdown:** When an enterprise holds dozens of separate accounts across domestic and global banking networks, analysts waste hours every morning manually logging in, grabbing files, and hand-stretching data sheets.
* **The 13-Week Forecasting Spreadsheet Nightmare:** Building liquidity look-aheads requires manually extracting data arrays out of different ERP modules, billing platforms, and payroll logs into massive, formula-heavy desktop workbooks that break easily.
* **Hidden FX Volatility Risks:** Operating across global boundaries without real-time, cross-system transactional currency visibility leaves the company vulnerable to un-hedged translation risks and flash margin erosion.
* **Opaque Bank Fee Overbillings:** Commercial bank statements hide compound transaction fees under cryptic code dialects, making manual error detection across thousands of monthly rows practically impossible.

## 10. What technologies improve it?
* **Modern Analytics Automation Pipelines:** Platforms that connect to bank SFTP sites or APIs, automatically extracting, cleaning, and translating messy BAI2/MT940 files into clear financial models.
* **Open Banking APIs & SWIFT Connectors:** Enterprise integrations that completely bypass manual portal interactions, routing cross-bank statement telemetry directly into data layers in real time.
* **Cloud Treasury Management Systems (TMS):** Multi-bank software architectures that serve as a single dashboard for global currency visibility, debt logging, and centralized payment rails.

## 11. What does AI change?
* **Predictive Liquidity Forecasting Models:** Machine learning models replace manual Excel guesses, parsing years of operational histories and seasonal patterns to automatically generate optimized cash predictions.
* **Autonomous Fraud Interception Audits:** AI monitors outgoing wires in real time, checking counterparty metadata and historical paths to instantly block suspicious payment rows before money leaves the account.
* **Intelligent Bank Fee Auditing:** Advanced algorithmic systems scan thousands of cryptic bank billing lines, instantly comparing them to negotiated service contracts to reveal hidden overbillings.

## 12. How do you discover opportunities?
Presales engineers can reveal high-value Treasury automation opportunities by targeting how analysts handle daily bank balance consolidation, tracking manual file manipulation during 13-week forecast setups, and exposing multi-bank visibility friction.

### Key Discovery Questions
* *"How many business hours does your team spend every morning logging into your various banking portals just to copy balances and calculate your net available cash position?"*
* *"Walk me through how your team currently builds your 13-week rolling cash forecast—how many different accounting sub-ledgers and billing CSV files must be manually stitched together in Excel?"*
* *"If you operate across international borders, how does your finance team track your transaction-level currency exposures to decide when to execute FX hedges?"*

### Pain Signals & Triggers

> **Pain Signal: Fragmented Multi-Bank Telemetry Slowdowns**
> * *Signal:* "We manage 40 different accounts across 4 separate global banks. Our analyst spends the first two hours of every single day logging into tokens, pulling BAI2 text files, and copy-pasting lines into Excel just to see our morning cash position."
> * *Automation Opportunity:* Implement a **bank statement automation pipeline**. The visual data workflow automatically checks bank SFTP repositories or connects via APIs every morning at 06:00, parses and cleans complex multi-source BAI2/MT940 text strings, aggregates the transactions into a standard master schema, and delivers a live cash position dashboard—wiping out hours of manual work before the analyst even logs on.

> **Pain Signal: Manual 13-Week Cash Forecast Stagnation**
> * *Signal:* "Building our weekly cash forecast takes days of spreadsheet engineering. We have to pull AP reports from NetSuite, pipeline files from Salesforce, billing projections from Stripe, and manually map them into a giant Excel model that frequently breaks."
> * *Automation Opportunity:* Deploy an **automated cash forecasting data pipeline**. The engine automatically pulls accounts payable due files, customer billing projections, and historical operational run-rates across all individual data silos via scheduled connections, executes the cross-system joining logic, and automatically compiles the look-ahead forecast rows—giving leadership instant visibility into cash runways.
